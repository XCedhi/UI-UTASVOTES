# UTASVotes

## Project Description
Project Title: UTASVOTES – Digital Campus Electoral System

Project Overview
UTASVOTES is a secure, scalable, and professionally designed web application aimed at revolutionizing the election process at the University of Technical and Applied Sciences (UTAS). The platform enables students to participate in departmental and university-wide elections from the comfort of their homes, eliminating long queues and promoting inclusivity, transparency, and efficiency. With a focus on high-level security, seamless user experience, and real-time engagement, UTASVOTES ensures a free, fair, and modern electoral process for the entire student body.

Key Features

Role-Based Access

Students: Vote for departmental and university leaders, view candidate manifestos, engage with campaign content, and participate in election-related discussions.

Candidates/Aspirants: Apply for positions through a professional onboarding process, pay application fees (in Ghana Cedis), upload profiles/manifestos, and share campaign updates.

Electoral Commission (EC): Manage elections, verify candidates, import voter data, monitor real-time results, and generate certified election reports.

Administrators: Oversee system operations, update fee structures, and ensure platform integrity.

Security & Authentication

Exclusive access for registered students via institutional emails (@cktutas.edu.gh).

Robust authentication mechanisms (OAuth, JWT) and encryption protocols to prevent fraud.

Tailored passwords distributed to verified voters after registration via Excel uploads.

Election Management

Multi-level voting (departmental → university-wide).

Real-time dashboard for EC to track vote counts, candidate progress, and election analytics.

One-click certified result generation with automated email dissemination to all student emails.

Candidate Onboarding

Streamlined application forms requesting constitutional requirements, profile pictures, and manifestos.

Integrated payment gateway (Stripe) for position-specific application fees (configurable by EC).

EC verification and approval workflow for candidates.

Engagement & Communication

Dedicated feed for election updates: manifestos, campaign messages, videos, and announcements.

Interactive features allowing students to comment, ask questions, and share ideas.

Notifications for important deadlines, candidate updates, and election results.

Professional UI/UX

Modern glassmorphism design (iOS-style glass effects), smooth animations, and responsive layouts.

Technical Stack

Frontend: React/Next.js (with Tailwind CSS for styling).

Backend: Node.js/Express.js.

Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL).

Authentication: Google Cloud Identity, OAuth 2.0.

Payments: Stripe Integration (Ghana Cedis).

Hosting & Deployment: Vercel (frontend), Google Cloud (backend).

Version Control: Git/GitHub.

AI Tools: Cursor AI & Claude AI (for development assistance).

Objectives

Provide a secure, accessible, and transparent digital voting platform.

Reduce physical barriers and logistical constraints in traditional elections.

Enhance student engagement through real-time election updates and interactive features.

Ensure compliance with institutional electoral guidelines and student rights.

Target Users

UTAS students (voters).

Student aspirants/candidates.

University Electoral Commission.

System administrators.

Expected Outcomes

Increased voter turnout and participation.

Streamlined election management and result dissemination.

A scalable model adaptable to other universities or institutions.

Note: This project prioritizes data privacy, ethical design, and non-infringement of student rights. All processes will adhere to UTAS electoral policies and Ghanaian data protection regulations.
